# 💊 ADR Identification and Assessment – Pharmacovigilance

## 🔍 Project Overview
This project focuses on identifying and assessing potential Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs) through the analysis of 18 fictional patient case scenarios. It demonstrates the basic principles of pharmacovigilance, including medication history review, reaction assessment, and safety documentation.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify suspected drugs and reported adverse reactions.
- Evaluate the timeline between drug administration and reaction onset.
- Assess dechallenge and rechallenge information.
- Review expectedness and seriousness of reported reactions.
- Document patient outcomes and potential drug-event relationships.
- Identify missing, unusual, or inconsistent case information.

## 🔬 Methodology
Each of the 18 cases was individually reviewed based on the available patient and medication information.

The assessment involved:
1. Suspected drug and adverse reaction identification.
2. Chronological assessment of reaction onset.
3. Dechallenge and rechallenge evaluation.
4. Expectedness and seriousness assessment.
5. Patient outcome documentation.
6. Preliminary causality assessment and data quality review.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pharmacovigilance case assessment principles

## 📊 Project Outcome
Successfully reviewed and documented all 18 case scenarios in an Excel workbook, including individual case assessments, preliminary causality interpretations, and data quality observations.

## 📚 Key Learnings
- Understanding the fundamentals of pharmacovigilance.
- Identifying and documenting potential ADRs.
- Interpreting dechallenge and rechallenge information.
- Understanding the importance of accurate safety data documentation.
- Recognizing the limitations of causality assessment.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
This project is intended solely for educational and practice purposes. All patient scenarios and identifiers are fictional. The assessments do not represent formal clinical causality determinations or regulatory safety submissions.
